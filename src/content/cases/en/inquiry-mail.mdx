## The scenario

A sales team receives 30–50 emails a day between website inquiries and requests from existing clients. Triage and routine replies eat 90 minutes daily, and logging the interaction tends to get pushed to "later" — which often means never.

## What gets automated

Inbound email is classified by AI into categories (quote request, spec question, sales pitch, other), and a reply draft is written in a tone matched to the category. The interaction is logged to the tracking sheet automatically. A person always reviews the content before anything is sent.

## Why it works

- 90 minutes of daily triage and drafting becomes a 15-minute review
- Fewer missed replies and faster first response times
- Logging gaps disappear, so the tracking sheet always reflects current status
